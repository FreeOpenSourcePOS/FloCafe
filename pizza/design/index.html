What the ticket shows

  • 80mm paper, 48 columns, printed here at actual column width. The same lines wrap at commas on 42-column printers.
  • Heading in bold with the quantity in front. The print layer adds 2x; the pizza text comes from one shared function.
  • Removals are upper case NO … lines under the pizza or half they belong to.
  • Halves are labelled 1ST: and 2ND:, with each half’s removals indented below it. Cook and cut share one line after both halves.
  • Deal pizzas are numbered 1) 2) 3) so the cook can tick them off, then WINGS: and the flavour.
  • Large short order number, order type, name and phone at the top. Delivery adds the address line.
  • Dips and drinks print as plain lines.
  • Optimises for a cook reading at arm’s length: one bold heading per item, every removal on its own upper-case line.
  • The same lines appear in the cart and in the “Prints as” boxes of the sheets, so what staff see is what prints.
  • Not drawn: the free-text note. It is FloCafe’s own special-instructions line, printed by the existing ticket code, not part of the pizza text.
